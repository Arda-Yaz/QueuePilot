# Remote access VPN

## [expired-profile] VPN profile renewal

If the VPN client reports an expired profile, open the company self-service portal on a trusted connection and download a fresh VPN profile. Import it into the VPN client and reconnect. Keep the previous profile until the new connection succeeds.

## [certificate-date] Certificate date check

If the VPN reports a certificate date error, turn on automatic date, time, and time zone in the device settings. Reconnect once the clock is synced. Send the error code and device time zone to the support operator if the error persists.
